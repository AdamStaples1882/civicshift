Digital Glossary

Understanding the terminology behind local government digital transformation, LGR, and Enterprise Architecture.

LGR (Local Government Reorganisation)

The process of restructuring local government authorities, often merging multiple district or borough councils into a single unitary authority to streamline services and reduce costs.

Enterprise Architecture (EA)

A conceptual blueprint that defines the structure and operation of an organisation. In public sector terms, it aligns IT systems and data with statutory service delivery goals.

TOGAF

The Open Group Architecture Framework. A high-level approach to design, which is typically modeled at four levels: Business, Application, Data, and Technology.

ArchiMate

An open and independent enterprise architecture modeling language to support the description, analysis, and visualization of architecture within and across business domains.
