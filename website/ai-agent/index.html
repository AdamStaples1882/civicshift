Meet your AI Service Agent

The Civic Shift Ai Team offers a reliable, policy-driven digital worker that operates 24/7 on your website.

  • Answers common queries out-of-hours using your approved, public knowledge base.
  • Routes residents to the exact right form or team, handling initial triage with strict escalation rules.

Frequently asked council questions

Is this just a chatbot?

No. Standard chatbots use rigid decision trees that fail when queried dynamically. This is an AI service agent backed by governance rules, capable of understanding context and triggering complex workflows.

Will it replace staff?

No. It is designed to act as a digital filter. By handling the 40% of queries that are simply "routine signposting," it reduces avoidable contact and releases front-line staff to tackle complex casework that requires a human.

Does it access resident records?

By default, no. It operates on your publicly available domain data. Deep back-office lookups (e.g., checking specific housing benefit status) only happen on the Enterprise tier with secure, credentialed API integration.

What about digital exclusion?

The agent is an optional channel. It absorbs demand from the digitally confident majority, thereby reducing phone queues for vulnerable residents who genuinely need to speak to an officer.

How do we measure success?

We baseline your current costs and call volumes in Week 1. You receive a monthly dashboard showing containment rate, escalated volumes, and specific knowledge gaps where residents get stuck.
