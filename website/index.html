The Critical Challenges UK Councils Face Today

Local government IT and digital leaders operate under immense fiscal and political scrutiny. We solve the acute pain points that cause traditional programmes to stall.

⚠

Financial pressures require cashable, audited savings. Councils can no longer afford speculative digital experiments that fail to deliver measurable operational efficiencies.

⏱

High-Stakes LGR Cliff-Edges

Vesting Day deadlines are legally binding. Merging authorities risk catastrophic failure if revenues, benefits, and safeguarding systems are not guaranteed for Day-1 continuity.

Crippling Legacy Vendor Lock-In

Decades-old Northgate, Civica, Idox, and Capita contracts drain millions annually while holding council data hostage behind rigid proprietary architectures.

Underutilised M365 Investments

Authorities pay for comprehensive Microsoft 365 E3/E5 licenses, yet continue spending hundreds of thousands on third-party SaaS tools for simple forms and workflow tracking.

📞

Front-Door Demand Spikes

Contact centres and caseworkers are drowning in avoidable calls and manual data re-entry, leading to rising citizen frustration and chronic planning/housing backlogs.

⚖

AI Confusion vs Public Scrutiny

Councils want AI efficiency but fear ethical breaches, GDPR violations, hallucinated public advice, or lack of clear CDDO-aligned algorithmic governance.
