Supporting Local Government Reorganisation

Local Government Reorganisation (LGR) is one of the most complex and time-pressured digital challenges facing councils today. Merging systems, data, teams and services across multiple authorities requires experienced, calm leadership — at pace.

I work with district, borough and county councils to plan and deliver the digital elements of LGR: from data migration and CRM consolidation to operating model redesign and staff capability building. I bring the hands-on delivery experience to get it done.
