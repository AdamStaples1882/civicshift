Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Platinum "Problem Ownership" package work? +

In the Platinum tier, Civic Shift does not act as passive advisors. We step in with lead Enterprise Architects and Transformation Directors who assume direct end-to-end accountability for solving the programme's roadblocks—whether that is negotiating stalled supplier contracts, establishing the Solution Design Authority, or guaranteeing Day-1 LGR statutory continuity. Milestones are directly linked to deliverables and cashable MTFS savings.

What procurement frameworks can councils use to contract Civic Shift? +

Civic Shift can be procured directly under local authority standing orders for rapid diagnostic work, via Crown Commercial Service frameworks (including G-Cloud 14 and Digital Outcomes & Specialists), or through established public sector prime contractor partnerships.

Are all consultancy packages outside IR35? +

Yes. All of our Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum engagements are contracted on an outcome-based Statement of Work (SoW) model with clearly defined milestone deliverables, fully compliant with off-payroll working (outside IR35) regulations.

How do you help councils "get the best out of Microsoft 365"? +

Most local authorities already pay for comprehensive Microsoft 365 E3/E5 licenses but continue paying third-party SaaS vendors hundreds of thousands of pounds for departmental databases and form builders. We configure the Power Platform (Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI) and modern SharePoint information architecture to replace these costly subscriptions with secure, council-owned solutions.
