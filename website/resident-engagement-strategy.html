INCLUSIVE CITIZEN COLLABORATION

Resident Engagement Strategies & Citizen Experience

Resident Engagement Strategies empower UK local authorities to build inclusive, omnichannel consultation frameworks, drive digital channel-shift, and rebuild community trust. They satisfy statutory consultation mandates while reducing avoidable resident complaints and contact centre volume.

MOVING BEYOND SURVEYS

Transforming Citizen Engagement into Actionable Insight

Too many public consultations are static, one-way compliance exercises that leave residents feeling ignored and elected members blindsided by public backlash. Furthermore, digital-only strategies risk excluding vulnerable citizens who need assisted digital access.

Civic Shift designs omnichannel resident engagement frameworks that combine modern digital consultation platforms with assisted face-to-face channels. We turn raw resident sentiment into closed-loop feedback dashboards that inform Cabinet decisions and accelerate service adoption.

Core Engagement Capabilities

  • ✓ Omnichannel Consultation Strategy: Blending digital platforms, hybrid townhalls, and assisted channels.
  • ✓ Digital Inclusion & Accessibility: Ensuring non-digital and vulnerable residents retain full access.
  • ✓ Closed-Loop Citizen Feedback: Showing residents how their input shaped policy and service redesign.
  • ✓ Executive Sentiment Reporting: Real-time Power BI customer satisfaction monitoring for Leadership.
