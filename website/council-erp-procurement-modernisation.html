AVOIDING VENDOR LOCK-IN

Why Traditional Council Software Procurement Fails

Local authorities frequently find themselves held hostage by legacy software vendors charging exorbitant annual maintenance fees for obsolete software. When procurement cycles arrive, councils are often outmatched by aggressive supplier sales tactics, resulting in vague contracts, scope creep, and multi-year implementation delays.

Civic Shift represents the council's interest exclusively. We act as your independent commercial and architectural client-side advisor—authoring rigorous Statements of Requirements (SoR) on Crown Commercial Service (CCS) G-Cloud 14 and Digital Outcomes & Specialists (DOS) frameworks, running competitive tender evaluations, and enforcing milestone-driven contracts that hold software vendors accountable.

Commercial Procurement Deliverables

  • ✓ Vendor-Neutral Tender Specifications: Outcome-based technical requirements preventing single-supplier bias.
  • ✓ CCS Framework Procurement: Compliant procurement execution via G-Cloud 14 and DOS.
  • ✓ Price & License Benchmarking: Robust commercial challenge saving councils hundreds of thousands of pounds.
  • ✓ Migration & Go-Live Governance: End-to-end client-side assurance de-risking statutory data cutovers.
