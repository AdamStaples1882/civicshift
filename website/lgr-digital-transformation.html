UNITARY CONVERGENCE PRACTICE

Local Government Reorganisation (LGR) Transformation

Local Government Reorganisation (LGR) Digital Transformation is the strategic alignment, systems rationalisation, and technical cutover required when district, borough, and county councils merge into a unitary authority. It guarantees Day-1 statutory service continuity and secures long-term MTFS efficiencies.

DAY-1 STATUTORY ASSURANCE

De-Risking the Most High-Stakes Public Merger

Merging councils face legally binding Vesting Day deadlines. If Council Tax billing fails, benefits payments stall, or safeguarding records become inaccessible during cutover, the financial and human costs are catastrophic.

Civic Shift brings battle-tested transitional architecture, cutover runbooks, and multi-supplier decommissioning strategies. We ensure your Joint Committees, Chief Executives, and S151 Officers have total visibility and confidence from day one.

Core LGR Transformation Areas

  • ✓ Day-1 Statutory Continuity: Fail-safe cutover plans for revenues, benefits, and social care.
  • ✓ Systems Rationalisation: Consolidating duplicate Northgate, Civica, Idox, and Capita instances.
  • ✓ Target Operating Model (TOM): Unified service delivery architecture for the new unitary body.
  • ✓ MTFS Efficiency Realisation: Eliminating duplicate licensing to release multi-million savings.
