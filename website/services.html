OUR CONSULTANCY PRACTICE AREAS

Local Government Digital & Architecture Services

Helping UK councils and unitary authorities navigate statutory complexity, LGR restructuring, and legacy technology sprawl with calm, institutional-grade architecture and execution.

CORE PRACTICE PILLAR 1

Enterprise Architecture Services (TOGAF, SPARX EA & ArchiMate)

We establish robust, repeatable architectural frameworks tailored specifically to UK local government statutory services. By modeling your business capabilities, applications, data objects, and technology infrastructure in SPARX Systems Enterprise Architect using TOGAF 10 and ArchiMate 3.2, we give decision-makers a single source of truth to de-risk investments and eliminate shadow IT.

SPARX EA Repository Setup & Metamodel

Design and build of centralized, multi-user SPARX EA repositories configured with custom public sector ArchiMate metamodels and tagging conventions.

Service Cluster Target Operating Models (TOM)

Complete business and application architecture mapping across council directorates: Adult Social Care, Children's Services, Revenues & Benefits, Housing, Planning, and Environment.

Solution Architecture Reviews & SDA Governance

Establishing and co-chairing Solution Design Authorities (SDA). We author formal Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) to govern major procurements and technical trade-offs.

CORE PRACTICE PILLAR 2

Microsoft 365 & Power Platform Acceleration

Most councils only scratch the surface of their existing Microsoft 365 E3/E5 investment, continuing to pay high recurring SaaS licenses to third-party vendors for simple forms and tracking tools. We help you get the absolute best out of M365 by building secure, enterprise-grade low-code solutions on the Microsoft Power Platform.

Custom Power Apps Development

Rapid design of Model-Driven and Canvas Power Apps replacing legacy paper, clunky spreadsheets, and bespoke departmental databases with modern, mobile-friendly forms.

Power Automate Process Orchestration

Automating cross-departmental handoffs, approval workflows, and data syncing between Microsoft 365, SharePoint, Dataverse, and legacy council line-of-business systems.

Power BI Executive & S151 Dashboards

Real-time corporate performance, MTFS budget monitoring, and statutory KPI dashboards built for Chief Executives, Directors of Finance, and Cabinet Member scrutiny.

CORE PRACTICE PILLAR 3

AI Strategy, Roadmaps & Autonomous Agents

Local authorities cannot afford speculative AI experiments. We deliver practical, ethical Council AI Roadmaps aligned with Central Digital and Data Office (CDDO) and GDS standards. We deploy production-grade Autonomous AI Agents that solve two critical pressure points: curbed front-door resident contact and accelerated officer casework.

Council AI Readiness & Governance

Algorithmic transparency frameworks, GDPR/data ethics assessments, and risk classification matrices ensuring elected members and SIROs have complete confidence in AI deployment.

Front-Door Resident Triage Agents

Intelligent conversational agents resolving high-volume, repetitive citizen enquiries (waste, council tax, planning FAQs), releasing frontline customer service teams for vulnerable residents.

Officer Casework Copilots

Internal AI assistants that summarize complex planning histories, synthesize social care case notes, and automate statutory report drafting under human-in-the-loop control.

CORE PRACTICE PILLAR 4

LGR Convergence & Transformation Delivery

Merging multiple district, borough, and county councils into a new unitary authority is one of the highest-risk endeavours in public administration. We provide the architectural backbone and transitional governance that guarantees Day-1 service continuity while locking in long-term MTFS efficiencies.

Multi-Council Systems Rationalisation

Auditing and de-duplicating disparate ERPs, revenues systems (Northgate, Civica), planning tools (Idox), and social care platforms (Liquidlogic) into a unified application roadmap.

Day-1 Statutory Continuity Assurance

Rigorous cutover roadmaps ensuring tax billing, benefits payments, safeguarding hotlines, and web portals remain fully functional from the first minute of vesting day.

Programme Turnaround & Recovery

When an LGR or major CRM programme is stalled, over-budget, or blocked by supplier disputes, our senior directors step in to reset governance, unblock teams, and drive delivery.
