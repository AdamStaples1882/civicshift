Cyber Resilience & Local Gov CAF Compliance

Council Cyber Resilience & Local Gov CAF Compliance provides institutional security architecture de-risking council services from ransomware, supply chain attacks, and cloud exposures. We implement NCSC Cyber Assessment Framework (CAF) and DLUHC Cyber Health standards to safeguard statutory continuity.

THE THREAT LANDSCAPE

Why Local Government is the Primary Target for Cyber Ransomware

Recent catastrophic cyber incidents across UK local authorities demonstrate that councils cannot treat security as an IT bolt-on. Stolen resident data, frozen housing payments, and offline social care records cost millions of pounds in recovery expenses and inflict irreversible reputational damage.

Civic Shift translates central government security mandates into practical, architected defence. Benchmarked against the NCSC Cyber Assessment Framework (CAF) and DLUHC Cyber Health baseline, we implement Zero Trust network segmentation, immutable air-gapped backups, automated Microsoft Defender/Sentinel telemetry, and board-level disaster recovery playbooks.

Cyber Resilience Deliverables

  • ✓ NCSC CAF Audit & Gap Analysis: Rigorous assessment against the 4 CAF objectives and 14 principles.
  • ✓ Zero Trust & Identity Architecture: Conditional access, MFA enforcement, and privileged access management (PAM).
  • ✓ Air-Gapped Immutable Backups: Ensuring Day-1 service recovery is technically guaranteed even after total ransomware breach.
  • ✓ Audit Committee & Cabinet Playbooks: Non-technical executive risk matrices and incident response protocols.
