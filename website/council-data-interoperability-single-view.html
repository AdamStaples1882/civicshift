DATA ARCHITECTURE & INTEROPERABILITY

Data Interoperability & Single View of the Resident

Council Data Interoperability breaks down proprietary vendor silos across social care, housing, and revenues. We architect modern integration layers (IPaaS), Master Data Management (MDM), and Caldicott-compliant citizen records aligned with GDS Local standards.

THE SILO BOTTLENECK

Why Council Data Silos Put Vulnerable Residents at Risk

In most UK local authorities, a resident may be known to Adult Social Care, owe Council Tax in Revenues, be on the Housing waiting list, and have children in local schools—yet none of these systems talk to one another. Frontline caseworkers waste thousands of hours manually re-keying data, while vulnerable citizens slip through institutional blind spots.

Civic Shift architects modern, secure integration platforms (IPaaS) on Microsoft Azure and open APIs. We implement Master Data Management (MDM) solutions that safely match citizen identities, eliminate duplicate records, and give caseworkers a holistic 360-degree Single View of the Resident while strictly adhering to Caldicott Guardian and GDPR principles.

Core Data Deliverables

  • ✓ 360° Single View of Resident: Consolidated identity matching across statutory lines of business.
  • ✓ Enterprise IPaaS Architecture: Event-driven integration fabric replacing fragile point-to-point batch files.
  • ✓ GDS Local Standards Alignment: Open data schemas that prevent future proprietary vendor lock-in.
  • ✓ Caldicott & DSA Governance: Data Sharing Agreements and Information Asset Register (IAR) compliance.
