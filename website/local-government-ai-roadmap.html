Local Government AI Roadmaps & Council AI Agents

Local Government AI Roadmaps & Council AI Agents provide UK authorities with an ethical, CDDO-compliant strategy and production-ready conversational agents. They deflect avoidable front-door resident contact and accelerate caseworker research without algorithmic hallucination risk.

BEYOND THE HYPE: SAFE PUBLIC AI

Real Productivity Gains with Zero Hallucination Risk

Local authorities cannot risk deploying erratic chatbots that invent statutory planning advice or violate citizen data privacy. Yet doing nothing leaves contact centres overwhelmed by predictable queries while officers spend hours manually compiling case notes.

Civic Shift designs pragmatic, CDDO-compliant AI roadmaps and deploys two proven autonomous agent architectures: one that deflects front-door demand by resolving high-frequency citizen questions, and one that assists internal officers by synthesizing planning histories and case documentation.

Council AI Architecture Features

  • ✓ Front-Door Triage Agent: Resolves waste schedules, council tax FAQs, and licensing status 24/7.
  • ✓ Officer Casework Copilot: Synthesizes multi-year planning records and drafts committee summaries.
  • ✓ CDDO & GDS Ethics Alignment: Algorithmic risk matrix, full audit logging, and SIRO sign-off.
  • ✓ Human-in-the-Loop Controls: Sensitive requests automatically routed to qualified human caseworkers.
